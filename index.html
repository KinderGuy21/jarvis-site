JARVIS

A personal, single-user voice and chat assistant.

JARVIS is a private assistant built and run by Alon Beraha for his own use. It runs on his own computer at home and answers him by voice and on Telegram.

With his permission, JARVIS connects to Alon's own Google account so it can:

  • Gmail: search and read his email, and send an email when he asks it to.
  • Google Calendar: read his calendar and add, change or remove events when he asks.
  • Google Drive: search and read his files (read-only).

JARVIS is not a public product or service. There is no sign-up, it is not offered to anyone else, and it does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
